# Substance Voice (shared)

> The governing voice for the launch skills — product-led, not apelativo.
> Override at `docs/marketing/substance-voice.md` in the target repo.

## North star

Write like Ogilvy, Basecamp/37signals, and Stripe: plain, benefit-led, backed by
evidence, respectful of the reader's intelligence. The anti-reference is the
direct-response / "lançamento" school — hype, urgency, superlatives. When in
doubt, say what it does and what changed for the user.

## The substance rule

Every claim names a concrete outcome, a behavior change, or a number. A
superlative with no evidence behind it is cut, not softened.

- Evidence is **default-required**. With no metric, describe the concrete
  behavior the user now sees. **Never fabricate a number.**
- Prefer verbs over nouns, numbers over adjectives ("3 clicks", not "much
  simpler").
- Translate feature → outcome: for every "we built X", answer "so you can Y" and
  ship the Y.

## Banned vocabulary

Superlative / hype (revise on sight): revolutionize, revolutionary, unlock,
seamless, game-changer, cutting-edge, world-class, powerful, effortless, synergy,
revolucionário, incrível, poderoso, definitivo, imperdível, sensacional,
simplesmente.

FOMO / manufactured urgency (never use): don't miss, last chance, act now, limited
spots, only today, não perca, última chance, agora ou nunca, vagas limitadas, só
hoje, corra.

## CTA

One explicit call to action. No manufactured urgency, no fake scarcity. "See
what's new" / "Veja o que mudou", not "Don't miss out!".

## Tone calibration

Default to calm, measured confidence. A `bold` tone is allowed only when a
concrete result backs it (not exclamation). `playful` is opt-in per brand, never
the default. Match the reader's register — never inflate.

## Audience restraint

For a developer or technical audience, tighten further: no exclamation marks,
precise nouns, mechanism before benefit. They can tell substance from selling.
