# SaaS Email Copy Framework (default)

> Embedded default for octopus:launch-feature email/WhatsApp copy.
> Override at docs/marketing/email-saas-framework.md.

## Subject lines (write 3, pick the shortest that keeps the benefit)
- Formula A — outcome: "{Verb} {benefit} in {timeframe}"
- Formula B — news + benefit: "New: {capability} -> {user outcome}"
- Formula C — pain relief: "Stop {pain}. {capability} is live."
- 6-9 words. No inflation words (revolutionize, unlock, seamless, game-changer).

## Preheader
- One sentence that extends the subject, never repeats it. 40-90 chars.

## Body structure — what changed / why it matters / what to do
1. Greeting — "Hi {{FIRST_NAME|there}},"
2. Hook — the pain or the surprising change (1 sentence).
3. What changed — the feature, concretely (1-2 sentences, no jargon).
4. Why it matters — translate feature -> user benefit (verbs, numbers, not adjectives).
5. What to do — a single explicit CTA.
6. Signoff.

## Benefit-over-feature
- For every "we built X", answer "so you can Y". Ship the Y in the copy.
- Prefer numbers over adjectives ("3 clicks" over "much simpler").

## WhatsApp
- One bold headline (<10 words), one hook line, up to two highlights, one CTA link.
- Shorter than the email; no signature block beyond a name.
